# Historical reference: March 2010

Phasebook recreates a coherent **March 2010 U.S. desktop Facebook** layout: the February homepage redesign together with the classic tabbed profile, before the December 2010 profile changes. This is an interpretation of period references, not a pixel-exact archival copy. All people and activity are fictional; Phasebook is not affiliated with Facebook/Meta.

## Sources and design decisions

- [Facebook: Next Evolution of Site Design, July 20, 2008](https://about.fb.com/news/2008/07/facebook-unveils-next-evolution-of-site-design/) — classic Wall, Info, Photos, Boxes profile tabs and Publisher.
- [Facebook: Who’s Sharing on Facebook?, March 4, 2009](https://about.fb.com/news/2009/03/whos-sharing-on-facebook/) — Wall, Publisher, feed stories and comments.
- [TechCrunch: Facebook Begins Rolling Out Latest Redesign, February 4, 2010](https://techcrunch.com/2010/02/04/facebook-begins-rolling-out-new-redesign-pics/) — left navigation, central search, Top News and Most Recent.
- [The Next Web: A Guide to Facebook’s Brand New Homepage, February 5, 2010](https://thenextweb.com/news/guide-facebooks-brand-homepage-handy-diagram) — annotated contemporary homepage reference.
- [Web Design Museum: Facebook in 2010](https://www.webdesignmuseum.org/gallery/facebook-in-2010) — preserved period screenshot.
- [Meta Engineering: Reducing markup size, September 1, 2009](https://engineering.fb.com/2009/09/01/web/reducing-markup-size/) — Like/Unlike and compact feed feedback.

The reconstruction uses a centered 980px desktop layout, approximately #3b5998 header, Lucida Grande/Tahoma/Arial text, mostly 11–12px typography, square 50px feed avatars, pale-blue flat comments, thin rules, a bottom chat bar, and dense navigation. Exact widths, ranking and icon placement are approximations because period screenshots differ across rollouts. Smaller screens use a compact adaptation; the historical target is desktop.

No Timeline, cover photos, Stories, reactions, threaded comments, Messenger branding, hashtags or modern cards. Photos use basic albums, groups use a simple membership and wall model, and conversations stay in the old bottom chat/inbox pattern.

## Intentional simulation additions

- Phasebook wordmark in place of Facebook branding.
- Log In changes the active synthetic profile; it never authenticates anyone.
- Any profile can be edited, and all records can be exported/imported through Manage this world.
- Top News is a deterministic local engagement sort; Most Recent is chronological. Neither is Facebook's ranking algorithm.
- Seed activity uses March 2010 dates. New activity uses the computer's actual time and shows explicit dates.
- Online indicators are a synthetic display convention, not presence detection.
- Profile/album photos were generated as one fictional 3×3 atlas, then sliced into bundled JPEG files. Two seed profiles reuse atlas portraits. They are not photos of real accounts.
- No ad delivery, external navigation, authentication, cloud storage, external API, or real social networking service.

- The Boxes tab contains simple read-only group and interest boxes as a local approximation of period application boxes.
